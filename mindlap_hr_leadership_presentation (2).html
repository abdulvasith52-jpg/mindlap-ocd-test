01 — Vision

Our purpose is to provide quality consultation to society.

Company motto
Where Mind Finds Comfort
Message to the team

This company is not only about numbers. It is about trust, service quality, and creating a professional experience that people remember.

02 — Growth target

We are moving from 6–7 sessions per day to 10 sessions per day as the first step.

Current reality

We already have a working base. The next stage is to make it stable, repeatable, and professional.

What growth needs

Structure, punctuality, ownership, consistent follow-up, and smooth internal coordination.

What changes today

Clear systems, better discipline, and one standard for how everyone works together.

03 — Culture

Office etiquette and working discipline will be followed from today onward.

Professional behavior
  • Respectful communication
  • On-time reporting and attendance
  • No gossip, conflict, or confusion culture
  • Neat workspace and responsible phone use
Why this matters

Discipline is not pressure. It is the foundation that keeps the team organized as the company grows.

Working time process must be consistent, clean, and understood by everyone.

Start and end discipline

Everybody should know the timing standards and follow them properly.

Break and handover flow

Work should not get disturbed because of unclear shift or handover behavior.

Response expectations

Internal response and client handling should happen within a reliable time frame.

07 — Performance culture

We are building a company where ownership matters.

What ownership means
  • Do not wait for repeated instructions
  • Take responsibility for outcomes
  • Bring solutions, not only problems
  • Support team goals, not individual confusion
How we measure growth

Sessions, follow-up quality, attendance, operational discipline, and team execution will matter more and more as the company grows.

Professional consultation creates confidence.
